# Copy to share with Liquid users and the ATG community

I'm trying something new: I'm giving away the backtesting engine I just used.

I packaged it so you can work through it with your AI. Download the folder, open **00_START_HERE.txt**, and paste everything in that file into your model. It will help you describe your strategy, ask any questions it needs, and guide you through setup and your first test.

The rest of the engine is in the same folder. Your AI needs file access and the ability to run Python; the prompt will help you get set up if something is missing.

This first version tests your rules in five synthetic market scenarios, with fees and slippage excluded. It's a way to explore how a strategy behaves before adding real-world trading costs. Synthetic results aren't proof that a strategy will be profitable.

Download: https://github.com/wheelieinvestor/liquid-strategy-lab/releases/latest/download/liquid-strategy-lab-ai-starter.zip

Start with **00_START_HERE.txt**. Tell the AI your idea, and work from there.
