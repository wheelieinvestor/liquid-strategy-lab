# Demo data and redistribution

`src/liquid_strategy_lab/data/btc-demo.csv` is authored synthetic data generated by `scripts/generate_demo_data.py`. It contains 24 hours of warmup and 32 hours of fictional price movements. It is distributed under the repository's MIT license. Rebuild it with `uv run --frozen python scripts/generate_demo_data.py`.

The shared-account events in `liquid_autonomous_trader/backtesting/fixtures.py` are likewise authored fixtures. Their Flow alerts, GEX inputs, market books, and Cramer classifications are fictional. Names of markets identify the simulated instruments and do not imply that an observed trade or real post occurred.

No personal trade history, private account export, original model archive, or third-party paid feed is bundled. Imported datasets stay local; their ownership and permissions remain with their respective providers. Receiving access to a data source does not automatically grant permission to republish it.

The public-data recorder is an optional advanced command that performs bounded market-data reads. Demo, configured local runs, portfolio examples, and the four included standalone stress families need no network. Installing dependencies is a separate online step.
